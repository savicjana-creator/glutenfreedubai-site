Why it exists

Gluten-free in Dubai is mostly guesswork.

Menus don't always say. Staff aren't always sure. The big review apps are global, thin on the Gulf, and hide the useful bits behind a subscription. So every meal out starts with the same anxious questions. Is the fryer shared? Is the sauce thickened with flour? Do they even know what celiac means?

This is one place that answers them, built for Dubai and kept honest by people who actually have to ask.

How places are rated

Three levels of safe
