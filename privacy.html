Gluten Free Dubai

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Using the guide

You do not need an account to browse Gluten Free Dubai. Saved places and the flag recording whether you have seen the introduction are stored on your device. We do not receive your saved-place list.

Location and maps

Location is optional and is requested when you use Near me. Coordinates are used to calculate distances and display your position; the guide does not send them to a Gluten Free Dubai server. Map imagery comes from OpenFreeMap and map scripts from cdnjs. Those services receive network information such as your IP address and requested map area when the map loads. External directions open in your chosen map service under its own policy.

Newsletter

Newsletter registration opens our MailerLite form. If you choose to subscribe and confirm your email, Gluten Free Dubai receives your email address to send recipes, local finds and community news. MailerLite processes subscription and email-delivery information on our behalf under its privacy policy. You can unsubscribe through the link in any newsletter, or contact us to ask about your subscription or deletion. Simply using the app, reporting a listing or sending an enquiry does not subscribe you.

Affiliate links and other services

Some clearly marked shopping links may earn Gluten Free Dubai a commission. The retailer or affiliate service handles attribution and purchases under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not process your payment details. Website, Instagram, phone, delivery and other external buttons open third-party services with their own data practices.

Reports and enquiries

Report and partnership forms prepare a draft in your email app. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the message. If you send it, we receive your email address and the information you include and use it to reply, review the listing or discuss your enquiry. Please do not include medical records, passwords or other unnecessary private details.

Your choices and contact

You can use the guide without location access or a newsletter subscription. You can remove saved places in the app and manage location permission in your device settings. For questions or deletion requests relating to information you have sent us, email dxbglutenfree@gmail.com.

Children and changes

The guide is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. We update this notice when the app’s data practices change. Gluten Free Dubai is a community guide, not medical advice; always discuss preparation directly with the venue.
