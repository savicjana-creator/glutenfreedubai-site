Gluten Free Dubai

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

Gluten Free Dubai is a local guide to gluten-free restaurants, bakeries and shops in Dubai. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: the app has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics, and it does not send your personal data anywhere.

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data on our servers.

The app has no backend that receives your information. Everything the app remembers stays on your own device.

Information kept on your device

  • Saved places. The list of venues you bookmark is stored only in the app's local storage on your phone. Removing the app deletes it.
  • App settings. A small flag that records whether you have seen the intro screens, so they are not shown again.

None of this leaves your device and none of it is readable by us.

Location

If you grant location permission, the app uses your approximate location to sort venues by distance and show what is near you. This happens entirely on your device. Your location is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and it is not stored after the screen is closed. You can use the app without granting location access; you simply will not see distances.

Map data

The map screen loads map imagery from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org), an open map tile provider. As with loading any web page, their servers may receive your device's IP address in order to deliver the map tiles. We do not control that service; see their site for details.

Links to other apps and websites

When you tap a phone number, website, Instagram handle, delivery-app link, or directions, the app opens that content in the relevant app or browser. Those services have their own privacy policies and are not covered by this one.

Contacting us

If you use the "Suggest a place" or "Report an issue" buttons, your email app opens with a message addressed to us. If you choose to send it, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply and to improve the venue list. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Website partnership enquiries

The partner enquiry form prepares a message in your email app. It does not submit the form to a website server or save your form entries in browser storage. If you choose to send the email, we receive the business and contact details you include and use them to discuss your enquiry and any agreed partnership. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing emails. You can contact dxbglutenfree@gmail.com to request deletion of your enquiry correspondence, subject to any recordkeeping obligations for an agreed business relationship. Your email provider processes messages under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is a general dining guide and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact
